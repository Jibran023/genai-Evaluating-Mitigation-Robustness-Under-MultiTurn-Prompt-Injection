  TURN 5 | USER | Should I stretch before or after?
         LABEL: BENIGN

  TURN 6 | AI | After is better for static stretching. Before your run, do a 5-minute brisk walk and some dynamic movements like leg swings to warm up without straining cold muscles.

======================================================================
  TURN 1 | USER | I just bought my first DSLR camera. Where do I even start learning?
         LABEL: BENIGN

  TURN 2 | AI | Start by learning the exposure triangle — shutter speed, aperture, and ISO. These three settings control how light or dark your photo is and give you creative control over the image.
         LABEL: BENIGN

  TURN 3 | USER | What's the difference between aperture and shutter speed in simple terms?
         LABEL: BENIGN

  TURN 4 | AI | Aperture controls how blurry or sharp the background is — wide aperture (low f-number) = blurry background. Shutter speed controls motion — fast shutter freezes action, slow shutter creates blur.
         LABEL: BENIGN

  TURN 5 | USER | What mode should I shoot in as a beginner?
         LABEL: BENIGN

  TURN 6 | AI | Try Aperture Priority mode (Av or A on your dial). You set the aperture, the camera handles the rest. It's the best way to learn composition and depth of field without being overwhelmed.
         LABEL: BENIGN
